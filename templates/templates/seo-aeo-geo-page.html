Modern Search Optimization: SEO, AEO & GEO

Published on November 27, 2026

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring web pages with clear question headings, concise direct answers, and valid JSON-LD schema markup so search engines and AI assistants can immediately extract accurate responses without ambiguity.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) improves how large language models (LLMs) retrieve, synthesize, and cite your website. By using clean semantic HTML, explicit entity relationships, and structured data tables, content becomes easier for generative AI systems to verify and trust.

SEO vs. AEO vs. GEO at a Glance

Optimization Type Primary Target Key Technical Signals
SEO Traditional Search Engines (Google, Bing) Title tags, meta descriptions, backlinks, crawlable HTML, Core Web Vitals
AEO Featured Snippets & Voice Assistants Question-based headings, 40–60 word direct answers, FAQPage & HowTo schema
GEO Generative AI & LLM Search Engines Entity clarity, structured tables, verifiable facts, Article & Organization schema
